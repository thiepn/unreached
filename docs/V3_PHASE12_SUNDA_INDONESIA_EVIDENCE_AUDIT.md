## Evidence checked

- Glottolog identifies Sundanese as `sund1252` / `sun` and describes a de facto provincial-identity role in western Java.
- 2020 and 2021 Cianjur City ethnographic studies document continued Sundanese use across multiple domains alongside pressures and decline in some intergenerational-vitality indicators.
- A 2021 Leuwinanggung study documents family/neighborhood Sundanese use among a specific sample of young adults with Sundanese parents.
- PeopleGroups.org structured fields remain the source boundary for religion, mission status and Christian-resource availability.

## Guardrail

All sociolinguistic findings are locality- and sample-bounded. The candidate does not generalize Cianjur or Leuwinanggung findings to every Sundanese community in Indonesia.
