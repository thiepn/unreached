# Why Unreached? — Display Rules

1. The heading is a question about access conditions, not an essential label for a culture.
2. Always present the section as evidence-backed synthesis.
3. Show claim type and certainty where it materially helps readers distinguish fact from interpretation.
4. Current legal, conflict, displacement, or religious-freedom claims must expose temporal context.
5. Link material claims to their actual source records.
6. Never infer causation from primary religion alone.
7. Never describe an entire people as spiritually resistant.
8. Never turn a rounded zero into `there are no believers`.
9. Never claim universal gospel ignorance from a mission-status classification.
10. Do not expose precise vulnerable-community locations.
11. Aggregate religious affiliation must not be written as uniform individual belief.
12. Multiple dimensions may be shown, but no dimension should be fabricated to make the profile look complete.
13. Level C material must visibly read as interpretation rather than fact.
14. Stale current claims must not be silently displayed as current.
15. Editorial sources remain distinct from imported statistical provenance.

The intended reader takeaway is: `these documented conditions help explain limited gospel access`, not `this people has a defective culture`.
