# U8 — Prayer Display & Safety Rules

1. Prayer prompts may be explicitly Christian and may ask for people to hear and respond to the gospel, believers to mature, churches to grow in faithfulness, workers to be sent, Scripture to become accessible, and communities to experience peace and justice.
2. A prompt must not imply that every member of a people group shares the same belief, behavior or political position.
3. People-specific factual assumptions must be grounded in reviewed U7 contextual claims.
4. Current conflict, legal, displacement or resource claims require freshness dates.
5. No prayer prompt may reveal covert believers, underground church locations, hidden missionaries, private legal cases, or precise sensitive ministry locations.
6. Restricted content cannot be published.
7. Scripture references must support the prayer theme rather than function as decoration or evidence for an unsupported factual claim.
8. The interface must never award XP, points, ranks, prayer scores, leaderboards or spiritual achievements.
9. Duration modes are optional pacing aids, not targets for spiritual performance.
10. The app does not record a public prayer history or imply that pressing a button proves prayer occurred.
11. Daily selection is deterministic and editorially curated; it is not framed as divine selection or spiritual ranking.
12. Country prayer links mean “reviewed prayer-ready peoples connected to this country,” not a claim that the country itself has a single spiritual condition.
