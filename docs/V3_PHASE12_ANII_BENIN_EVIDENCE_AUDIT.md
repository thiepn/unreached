# Phase 12 Anii of Benin Candidate - AI-Assisted Evidence Audit

**Status:** draft evidence package; not maintainer approval and not publication  
**Checked:** 19 September 2026  
**Target:** PG012305 / PEID 12305 / Benin / Anii (blo)

## Evidence boundary

The draft uses current PeopleGroups.org profile data for source-native mission fields and independent linguistic/sociolinguistic sources for human context. It does not use Joshua Project narrative content in the Phase 12 publication package.

## Source strengths

- Glottolog independently identifies Anii (`anii1245`, `blo`) in Benin and Togo.
- SIL's 94-page 2009 survey documents dialect comprehension, language use, attitudes, bilingualism, education and literacy in Anii-Akpe communities and explicitly cautions that its findings are historical.
- A 2024 Cambridge Journal of Linguistics article reports Bassila fieldwork and collaboration with Anii speakers involved in local publication and literacy work.

## Guardrail

Older survey findings are written as historical findings, not current universal conditions. Bassila-specific observations are not generalized to every Anii locality. Religion and GSEC remain provider-level aggregate classifications.
