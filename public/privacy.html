Privacy notice

Effective and reviewed 19 September 2026

Unreached is designed to work without an account. Local-only use is the default.

Browser-local data

Depending on the features you use, this browser may store Saved/prayer membership, private notes attached to people you intentionally keep, a bounded local prayer-memory trail from explicit “Record prayer today” actions, a bounded recent-route list, the latest prayer timestamp you explicitly record, optional sync metadata, a validated PeopleGroups.org snapshot in IndexedDB for offline resilience, and a bounded history of distinct PeopleGroups.org mission-source states observed on this device. A signed-in session may also keep a Cloudflare Access token in sessionStorage until the browser session ends.

Private notes are limited to 2,000 characters each. Prayer memory retains at most the latest 30 recorded prayer moments. Both can be deleted locally and neither is treated as mission-source or editorial content.

Optional private continuity

Authentication alone does not enable synchronization. You must separately choose to merge this device and enable sync. When enabled, the private service is limited to Saved/prayer membership and source-backed snapshots, the single latest prayer timestamp, revisions/deletion tombstones, mutation identifiers, and an account key derived by hashing the authenticated email. The verified email is used transiently for authentication but Unreached does not intentionally persist the plaintext email address in D1.

The private service is designed not to store private notes, prayer-memory history, recent browsing history, prayer counts or totals, streaks, scores, rankings, completion metrics, guided-session history, the PeopleGroups.org corpus, the browser's PeopleGroups cache, or device-local mission-source history.

External services

The public application is hosted through GitHub Pages. Live mission data is requested from PeopleGroups.org. Optional authentication and the private continuity service use Cloudflare. Those providers receive ordinary network/request information under their own privacy and security policies.

Analytics and advertising

Unreached does not currently implement its own analytics, advertising, profiling pixels, cross-site behavioral tracking, or prayer-performance telemetry. Infrastructure providers may maintain operational or security logs under their own policies.

Your controls

  • Use the application without an account.
  • Save or delete local private notes.
  • Clear the local prayer-memory trail and recent browsing list.
  • Enable or disconnect private sync explicitly.
  • Export server-held private continuity data.
  • Sign out and clear the session token.
  • Delete server-held private account data.
  • Reset a people profile's local mission-source history to its current observed state.
  • Clear browser site data to remove all device-local state.

Deleting server-held account data does not automatically erase local Saved/prayer data, private notes, prayer memory, recent browsing, or mission-source history. Local browser state remains until you clear it separately.

Mission-data cache

The validated PeopleGroups.org snapshot is a device-local resilience cache. It is not uploaded to the private continuity service and is not exposed by Unreached as a public dataset download or API.

Historical mission intelligence is stored separately as a bounded local log of distinct PeopleGroups.org states actually observed by this browser. Repeated identical states are de-duplicated. The history is not synced, stored on the Unreached server, included in server exports, or used to persist Joshua Project responses. Clearing browser site data removes it.

Changes

Material privacy changes must update this public notice and the release-policy checks before deployment.
